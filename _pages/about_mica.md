Micaela Santilli is a psychologist and behavioural science researcher externally affiliated with the Human-Centered Health AI group.

Micaela completed her degree in Psychology at Favaloro University in Buenos Aires, Argentina (2012–2017), and was awarded an MSc in Behaviour Change from University College London (2021–2022). Following her graduation, she has worked on a range of projects in behaviour change and mental health across consulting and research settings in Europe, Latin America, and Africa.

She has collaborated with the Human Behaviour Change Project, contributing to the development and validation of the Behaviour Change Intervention Ontology. She is currently part of the APRICOT team, focusing on improving the usability of this ontology. 

Moreover, since 2024, she has been involved in the GALENOS project, where she contributes to developing a mental health ontology aimed at harmonising and integrating data from multiple living systematic reviews related to psychosis, anxiety, and depression.
