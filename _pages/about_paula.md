Paula Muhr is a visual artist, Professor of Transdisciplinary Studies at the Brand University of Applied Sciences Hamburg, and external affiliated researcher with the Human-Centered Health AI research group.

Paula studied physics, theory of literature, and visual arts in Novi Sad, Belgrade, and Leipzig. She holds a Postgraduate Diploma from the University of Fine Arts in Leipzig (2009). She obtained her PhD in Art and Visual History from Humboldt University Berlin (2021), where she investigated active functions that diverse types of images played in generating medical knowledge of hysteria/functional neurological disorder from the late 19th to early 21st century. She was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for History of Art and Architecture, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (2022-2023), where she worked on the DFG project "Browser Art" and pursued her own research into the algorithmic reconstruction of images from sparse data in neuroimaging and black hole imaging. Between February 2024 and December 2025, Paula was with our group in its previous institutional home at the Institute for Implementation Science in Health Care at the University of Zurich, first as a postdoctoral researcher and subsequently as an affiliated researcher.

Paula is an active member of the History Philosophy and Culture (HPC) Working Group of the Next Generation Event Horizon Telescope (ngEHT) Collaboration, the transdisciplinary Thematic Research Network "Art Matters" at the Heidelberg Unuversity and, since January 2024, a visiting researcher at the Department for Social Studies of Science and Technology at the Technical University Berlin. Her research focuses on the knowledge-producing functions of new imaging and visualisation technologies across the natural sciences, from neuroscience and medicine to physics. Within the Human-Centered Health AI research group, she examines potential benefits, risks and societal challenges arising from AI systems.
