Martin Larralde is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Human-Centered Health AI group.

Following Bachelor’s degrees in Molecular Biology (2016) and Computer Science (2018) at ENS Paris-Saclay, Martin pursued a Master’s degree in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics (2019) at Université Paris-Saclay. Martin then entered the EMBL International PhD Program at EMBL Heidelberg to work on the secondary metabolism of the human gut microbiome with Dr. Georg Zeller. He moved to the Leiden University Medical Center with the rest of the group in 2024, before successfully defending his PhD thesis in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Shinishi Sunagawa at ETH Zürich.

Having worked on the genomics of bacterial metabolism during his PhD with classical statistical methods, where he also contributed multiple open-source software libraries for genomics data analysis, Martin joined our group in late 2026.

Martin works on advancing methods for AI models of biomolecules, with an emphasis on exploring natural product chemistry through the lens of metabolomics and on developing AI-driven methods for exploring bacterial metabolism, in the context of the StrOntEx and MetaboLinkAI projects.
