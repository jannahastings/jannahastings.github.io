The Human-Centred Health AI research group is looking for a talented and motivated Postdoctoral Researcher to join our interdisciplinary team working at the intersection of computational methods and health science, for the Wellcome Trust-funded GALENOS project. You will develop, improve, and apply state-of-the-art natural language processing (NLP) and time series modelling techniques to the published scientific literature on mental health, with the goal of accelerating evidence synthesis, identifying trends in research, and generating novel insights that can inform policy and practice. This is an exciting opportunity to do methodologically rigorous work with real-world impact.

Your specific tasks will include training and fine-tuning language models and time-series models, analysing research data, writing research publications, collaborating with a global interdisciplinary team, and contributing to the development of public-facing web interfaces for users to interact with models.

The project is for three years, full time, on-site at the Idiap Research Institute, Martigny, Switzerland.  Approximate start date: June 2026

View more information and [apply online](https://careers.werecruit.io/en/idiap/offres/postdoctoral-researcher-nlp-for-evidence-galenos-project-fbfd7f) by 30th April 2026.
