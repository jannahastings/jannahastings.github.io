Join us for the 35th anniversary of IDIAP! We will celebrate with an open day where we will showcase our research. The HCHAI group will be present with a poster about *AI, Social Media, and Health*.
View more information [here](https://www.idiap.ch/35/#program).

Our [poster](/assets/pdf/ENG_open_day_poster_2026.pdf) explores two aspects of AI & health: the consequences of AI-generated pictures for our body image and the use of social media and AI to explore physical health issues:

 <img src="/assets/img/ENG_poster_open_day26.png" alt="IDIAP Open Day Poster" style="width: 100%;">

The AI & Physical Health part of our poster is largely based on our project [SocialMediAI](https://hastingslab.org/projects/8_project/). We are using LLMs to understand how users online interact with health content such as menstruation content. Our most relevant paper can be accessed [here](https://ebooks.iospress.nl/doi/10.3233/SHTI250940).

The AI & Mental Health aspect of our poster is largely based on our [UnRealBody](https://hastingslab.org/projects/4_project/) project, where we created a video intervention to inform young people about the dangers of over-idealised AI-generated images online. The video can be found [here](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/365bRI3eeP0), a podcast (in German) about the project can be found [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rt3Nni0eqCo). The publication will follow soon, so stay tuned!
