We study the complex interplay between human health and help or information-seeking behaviours, large language models and social media.

In particular, we are interested in how LLMs can support under-served conditions such as women's health: in this project, we are applying LLMs to explore help-seeking and information-seeking behaviour around women's health on social media.

We are also interested in monitoring and mitigating the complex problem of health misinformation on social media and in LLMs.
